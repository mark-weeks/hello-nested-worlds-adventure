**On puzzles:** world-reading means the answer comes from the node itself, from its own properties and its place in the hierarchy, not from a generic anagram, cipher or number pattern (the decode family). 5. Are external AI-agent players in scope for beta, are they ranked alongside humans, and can they be reported and suspended like humans? (Proposal: yes to reporting, never jurors.)
6. What dollar ceiling for the beta, and what daily cap per player?
7. Self-policing: how big is a jury, how many distinct reports open a case, who can suspend or ban (you only, or juries too), and what are the appeal rules?

9. Cohort basics: who is the research lead, which devices and dates, and what is the minimum age for testers?
10. Permanent history: should a chronicle trace, a recap, or other stored history label the actor human or agent? The chronicle covenant (AGENTS.md) leaves that trace unlabeled, and live presence may distinguish. C4 and A1 do not require history labels until you decide. The owner decided that neither stored history nor live presence labels the actor human or agent, with the guardrails in A1 in their place. Stored history needs no change, but served history does: `/chronicle` still serves `agent` and `persona` keys in cast rows' data, and its `actor` field is a credential hash for a keyed human but a cast name for a cast member's act ([2026-11-06 evaluation](../evaluation/2026-11-06-next-build.md), finding 6). The implementing PR must project those signals out of what is served without touching stored rows; the stored `agent` key stays, because the agent-solve covenant and the cast's addressability depend on it. Dropping the live labels, and the covenant's live-presence wording with them, is the structural-parity work.
