## Admission gate

Record the selected commit, venue, devices, facilitator/operator, session dates,
world status, retention/deletion statement and enabled routes before admission.
Use a separate evidence record for each requirement. Changes of scope require a
fresh applicability check, not the completion of every historical inventory item.

The Watch is 2–3 newcomers in explicitly disposable staging, with the facilitator
available throughout. The Cohort is the 8–12 person continuing-world study, with
seven-day observation windows. **The cohort starts only after a recorded watch**.
The owner supplied the three additional cohort requirements in version 4 of
“Enfolded · decisions on open PRs #173, #174, #175 · 15 Nov 2026” in the working
chat; the [scope record](../evaluation/2026-11-14-study-friction.md#cohort-requirements)
records that source and their text. Before the cohort:

1. A report reaches a person within 24 hours; threats or doxxing have a same-day
   path; someone is named on duty each day.
2. The cohort runs on an exact, labelled release that passed every automated test.
3. Every full-checklist item applies unless the owner excuses it in writing.

Stage labels do not excuse an item for the cohort. Record each written exception
with its item ID and the owner's decision; the narrower Watch column does not
supply an exception.
No temporary world silently becomes a promised continuing world by changing its label.

## Study measurements

Run [protocol 2](../evaluation/2026-09-12-pilot-protocol.md). Observe curiosity,
orientation, attempted actions and consequences, puzzle interest, collaboration,
useful/unprompted return and operational timings, including unsuccessful sessions.
The newcomer watch supplies initial evidence; seven-day returns come from the
cohort. Proposed percentages remain proposals until adopted. Automated checks do
not substitute for observed motivation or comprehension.

to PR #175; this checklist changes no standing agent or verification rule.

## Go/no-go gate

For the newcomer watch, use the Watch column of the eight entry requirements
against the actual session record. Every applicable requirement needs evidence;
a justified not-applicable decision must name the selected scope. A historical
Partial inventory status is neither a pass nor a veto. Puzzle-interest ratings,
useful return and other study measurements are not prerequisites to observe the
people who will supply them. Separate deployment and invitation authority remains.

A cohort cannot begin until the watch has happened and its observations, defects,
fixes and resulting admission decisions are recorded. It also needs the Cohort
column, the checked 24-hour reporting/same-day urgent path and daily duty coverage,
an exact labelled release that passed every automated test, and every full-checklist
item unless the owner excuses that item in writing. Record the written exceptions
alongside the admission evidence. The newcomer watch does not clear continuing-world
backups, consent, provider or spend decisions.
