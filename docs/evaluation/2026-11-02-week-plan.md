Three planning lenses each proposed a week from that evidence:

- **Critical path** to the first invited cohort.
- **Irreversibility and harm**: what cannot be retrofitted, or harms someone
  if it is absent when the first outside player arrives.

against the code**: is it already done, does the scope fit the files and both
clients, and what dependency is hidden. The other checked **covenants, one-way
doors and owner gates**.

## The chosen week

Four pull requests, listed in the order they should be reviewed:

1. **Integrity.** Latch the invite gate and make it fail closed (F4). Close an
   open socket when its credential stops being live (F5). Key chat and move
   buckets by credential (F7). Remove `GET /players` (F8). No migration and no
   held decision. It goes first because it repairs a box already treated as
   done (E4) and the only sanction that exists today (revoke), and the later
   safety work builds on it: a suspension must close open sockets within a
   minute, and the latch stops "suspend everyone" from reopening the world.
2. **Server-owned agent identities.** `/agent` and `/observe` use fixed names.
   `/agent/voice` answers only roster names and gives the authored quiet line
   for any other. Observer and Scout are reserved, pending owner confirmation
   (packet decision 7). This is the one-way door (F9) that agents can close
   alone, and it corrects the checked S1 box.
3. **This documentation pull request.** The decision packet, the
   newcomer-watch kit, this audit, and the checklist corrections. The owner is
   the bottleneck. S5 becomes irreversible at the first production chat line,
   the newcomer watch can start before Fly only if the venue is ruled, and the
   October decisions lapsed without a packet in front of the owner.
4. **Reversible suspension and ban.** An additive migration with insert-only
   sanction and lift tables, an owner-only CLI, and enforcement through the
   live-credential lookups (F6). G5's exit criterion cannot be met with revoke,
   and no held decision blocks it: G5 names the owner as sole reviewer. The
   migration door needs the owner's sign-off at merge.

Where the lenses agreed and split. All three put a decision document first.
Critical path and irreversibility both put socket revalidation and suspension
in the week. The skeptics added the gate latch, per-credential buckets and the
`/players` removal as missed candidates, and moved agent identities from a
stretch item to committed work. Player learning argued against the roughly
eight agent-days of safety work this week and for a watch kit; the week takes
its kit and defers the block and report halves of the safety stack, which is
where its argument holds: a facilitator-present watch has nobody to block, and
report evidence depends on S5.

Why four and not more: the merge record suggests that owner review, not agent
capacity, is the limit (see the contrarian finding), so the week carries one
migration and three pull requests without one.

S1's fail-closed moderation (#119, #124, #126) was a prerequisite the owner

done.

Consequence for this week: keep pull requests small and few, put the decisions
in front of the owner first, and give the owner a way to start player contact
(the local watch) that does not wait on Fly.
