How to Play

Enfolded: a living multiverse of eleven nested scales

You are an explorer in a world that was here before you arrived and keeps moving after you leave. Everything you do — every word, every act, every solved puzzle — becomes part of the world's permanent chronicle, and every player and agent who comes after you builds on it.

Your invite link opens the map view — the whole world as a living tree. Once you're oriented, there is a second way in: the scene view renders each place as a scene you stand inside, with the same world, the same key, and the same company.

The world

Reality is nested eleven deep: Multiverse → Universe → Galaxy → Planetary System → Planet → Region → Room → Object → Molecule → Atom → Subatomic Particle. Every place is unique — its name, its properties, its one-line aspect, and its generative art belong to it alone. Descend into children, climb back up, and watch the scenery change register as you cross scales.

What you can do

DoWhat happens
ExploreStep through passages. Badges hint at what's below: puzzles, danger, locked ways, other presences.
SpeakTalk to any place — it answers in character, and it remembers. Return later and it knows you. You can also address agents whose traces you find.
SolveCrack a node's puzzle. Attempts pool across every player present — solving is co-op by default.
ActChoose or combine actions native to the scale you are visiting. Preview their consequences before acting.
ChatTalk to other explorers in the same world seed.
ChronicleRead the world's full history, era by era — everything everyone ever did here.
WaybackScrub the place you're standing from its born state to the present. You see the past through today's art and, if you choose to listen, today's sound.

A spectrum of actions

You can tend, transform, create a mark, unsettle a pattern, or combine actions for a different result. Open Act beside Speak and Puzzle. Nothing is selected for you. The possibilities depend on the scale and its current conditions.

Choose an action to attempt it. The labeled action commits directly. Open Combine actions to choose up to four ordered actions, then attempt that combination. Each step meets the conditions when it takes effect. An action can leave no new material change; other steps can still take effect.

Describe an intention in the same Act surface. Submitting it authorizes the attempt. Naming actions in order works without a model. With a connected model, describe your purpose in your own words. Only material ambiguity about the action, target, scope or order asks you to revise it. Unsupported purposes or constraints remain unsupported; they never silently become a nearby action.

Acceptance records your attempt, not a promised result. Cosmic actions take time. Another traveler may change relevant conditions while you wait. Actions still echoing distinguishes accepted attempts, pending consequences and observed outcomes. It names places only after consequences arrive. The chronicle preserves what actually happened, including attempts that left no new material change.

If a reply is lost, Recover earlier attempt retrieves its receipt, including after a reload. It does not perform the action a second time. Once the receipt is recovered, choosing an action again makes a new attempt.

Every player decides their own actions. This includes human and AI players. You may speak with another traveler, propose an idea or invite their help. You cannot choose what they will do or act in their name. A conversation is not a command.

Consequences travel

Strong events — a solved puzzle, a scale verb — don't stay where they happen. Local attempts settle when you commit; cosmic attempts take time to mature. Then a disturbance can travel through enclosing places, meeting their current conditions. Other causal events ripple outward at world speed, one ring of scales every few seconds. Solve a puzzle in a Room and watch the Region settle moments later, then the Planet, then the Galaxy. The feed shows each arrival.

The art is the place

Every node draws its own generative art — a pure function of what it is and what has happened to it. Causal pressure saturates the palette and shakes the geometry; a stabilized place wears a halo; corruption glitches the composition; every recorded interaction etches a trace mark. If a place looks weathered, it has been lived in.

Hear the scale, hear the place

Each scale has its own sound: slow membrane tides, a vacuum chorale, a stellar procession, orbital percussion, broad planetary horn phrases, regional flute calls, enclosed harp echoes, close material gestures, molecular hockets, atomic bells and subatomic sparks. A related motif connects places without giving them the same backing track.

Within each scale, material and state shape the score. A metal object rings differently from wood; a tall room answers later; bonds and molecular geometry change the rhythm; ionization adds spectral activity. Actions can change these conditions, so the sound changes with them. Listen and adjust the volume beside the place name. Crossing scales introduces the new instrumentation promptly, while the old sound fades.

You are not alone

Other explorers share the world live — and so does a recurring cast of twelve wandering agents (Tessera, Aunt Entropy, The Locksmith, and the rest), each with their own temperament: tenders, destabilizers, scholars, wanderers. Each keeps to their favorite scales, has their own nerve for danger, and their own verbal habits. They walk the world between your visits, attempt puzzles under the same rules you play by, meet each other, and talk. Their conversations persist — ask a place what it overheard.

The Travelers panel shows everyone abroad right now — humans and the cast alike — and where they stand. Click a traveler to go to them (if they're deeper than your view reaches, the world unfolds to take you there). Solving is better together: everyone at a puzzle shares one pool of attempts, and one correct answer counts for all.

The scales think differently

The eleven scales are not the same game at different zoom levels. Each universe has its own physics — its declared law is real: in one, consequences barely travel; in another they tunnel through scales without touching them; in an Inverted universe, what should rise sinks. You learn a universe's temperament by acting in it and watching what your act does.

The great scales are slow. Kindle a galaxy or calibrate a universe and the act is recorded at once — but the change itself travels on a cosmic clock, settling minutes or hours later, often after you've gone. Plant, leave, return.

The smallest scale gives up on locality. Some particle pairs are entangled: solve one twin's puzzle and the other resolves in the same instant, however far apart they sit. And the cosmic scales pose questions about the fold itself — how many scales hold a place, how many lie within it. A name, read closely, is a map of its whole lineage.

Puzzles are enfolded too

Puzzles compose the way the world does. Constellations: a galaxy completes when every system it holds is resolved; a region, when every room is — and the completion is permanent, powerful, and credited. Split the work: co-op attempts already pool, so a cohort can light a constellation together. Lineage sigils seal deep places behind their whole ancestry — three marks read from three enclosing scales. Bonds ask an atom's puzzle one scale up, in the lattice that holds it. Wherever you stand, the way forward is to understand what enfolds you — or what you enfold.

Sealed rooms

Some rooms are sealed. A sealed room won't let you (or anyone) in until its lock is answered — and the key is never written on the door: it's a truth about the place that holds the room, readable one scale up. Speak the key from the threshold and the way opens — for everyone, permanently… until the world's slow entropy re-arms the room and the door seals itself again.

Nothing is lost

The world database is a continuous chronicle across every cohort of players. When you return, your positions, your conversations, and the marks you cut into the walls are still there. Open the Chronicle to read the eras before you — and to see the one being written around you right now. Open Wayback at any place to see how its substance, causal pressure, and accumulated wear reached the present. It is an interpretation, not a recording: the state is historical, while the art and sound are the world's current senses. Looking leaves no new trace.
